## Method
1. `get_value_creation` for the current plan and `get_deal` for the underwriting case.
2. Quantify the **EBITDA bridge**: entry EBITDA → exit EBITDA, broken into commercial (pricing, mix, new logos), operational (cost-out, procurement, footprint) and buy-and-build levers — each with an owner, timeline and £/$ impact.
3. Draft the **100-day plan**: the first-quarter workstreams, governance (board cadence), KPIs and reporting, and quick wins.
4. Tie the plan back to the returns case — which levers the IRR/MOIC most depends on.

## Firm conventions
- Every lever is owned, quantified and time-bound — no generic "improve operations".
- Governance and reporting are set on day one, not deferred.
