# Lightning demo — business audience — 10 minutes

The short cut of the [business walkthrough](DEMO-WALKTHROUGH-BUSINESS.md), which runs about
15 minutes. Same product, same claims, fewer stops. For a CEO, CFO, Managing Partner or
Managing Director's calendar slot — not for a technical review, which is the
[technical lightning cut](DEMO-LIGHTNING-TECHNICAL.md), and not for a deal team's daily
mechanics, which is the [PE-audience lightning cut](DEMO-LIGHTNING.md).

**Say this before you click anything:**

> "Everything you see is a demonstration book — invented companies, people and numbers. What
> I want you to watch for is not the data, it's the places this removes a manual task your
> team does today. Every saving I name is a specific task removed — there's no percentage or
> speed claim in this walkthrough that isn't tied to something concrete."

---

## The ten minutes

| # | Beat | What to do | What it proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Opening** · 1 min | Let the product load on Home. *"Not the features — the hours it gives back. Every saving today is one specific task removed."* | Sets the frame as operating capacity, not a feature tour. |
| **2** | **The day starts triaged** · 2 min | Point at the **daily briefing** paragraph, then **Needs attention**, then press **🔍 Evidence** on one line. *"This is the status assembly a chief of staff usually does before a Monday call — current the moment the page opens, and every line opens to its source."* | Removes the status-compilation step and the trust exercise of checking it. |
| **3** | **Deal flow** · 1 min | No new screen — narrate it. *"Every incoming signal gets screened against the fund's mandate automatically, before an analyst spends an afternoon on a company the fund could never buy. The same team gets a real look at more of the pipeline."* | Removes the analyst-hour cost of disqualifying a candidate, which is the actual lever on deal flow. |
| **4** | **The blank page is gone** · 3 min | Open **Helvetia Diagnostics** → **Papers**: the IC memo, deck and returns model are already drafted. Open **The case**: a Ready / Conditional / Not-ready verdict with named blockers. *"The job becomes improving a draft that already exists, and the status meeting becomes a decision meeting."* | Removes multi-day memo drafting and the pre-IC status meeting. |
| **5** | **Nothing forgotten** · 2 min | Back on Home, point at **Untracked follow-ups**. *"Every firm loses a promise made in a Teams thread that nobody wrote down. This surfaces it automatically — it doesn't chase, it notices."* | Removes the overhead of rediscovering a forgotten commitment weeks later. |
| **6** | **One system, source to exit** · 1 min | Open **Fund & Portfolio**. If relevant, open **Settings → Data sources → Your CRM**. *"Same governed record before and after close. If you already run a CRM, this connects to it — no double entry between the two systems."* | Removes the re-entry and reconciliation firms normally do between deal and portfolio systems. |

**The three-minute cut:** beats **2 → 4 → 6**. The briefing that assembles itself, the memo
that starts drafted, and the record that never needs re-entering. That is the whole story.

## Close

> "Productivity, deal flow and ease of use, in one sentence each. Every saving here came from
> removing a manual assembly step, not a speed promise. The team gets a real look at more of
> the pipeline because screening no longer costs an hour per candidate. And none of it needed
> a new tool, because it's Microsoft Teams your firm already runs all day."

**Then ask for something.** A working session with your deal team to map these against how
your firm runs them today, or a conversation about a pilot on one live deal.

## What it does for a firm's leadership

| What you showed | The overhead it addresses |
|---|---|
| The daily briefing, assembled automatically | The status-compilation work an associate or chief of staff does before every pipeline call. |
| **Needs attention**, with 🔍 Evidence on every line | The trust exercise of double-checking a status list before believing it. |
| Mandate screening, automatic per signal | The analyst-hour cost of disqualifying a candidate by hand — the real lever on deal flow. |
| The IC pack, drafted the moment a deal is created | The multi-day drafting exercise a committee memo normally requires. |
| **The case**'s Ready / Conditional / Not-ready verdict | The pre-IC status meeting that exists just to establish whether a deal is ready. |
| **Untracked follow-ups** | The commitment made in conversation that nobody wrote down and someone rediscovers weeks later. |
| **Fund & Portfolio**, same record before and after close | Re-entering and reconciling data between a deal system and a portfolio-monitoring system. |
| The CRM connector, pull in / push out automatically | The double entry between the pipeline system a firm already runs and the one it ran the deal in. |
| **Firm reporting**, certified or plainly draft | Rebuilding an investor report from scratch every quarter. |
| Runs inside Microsoft Teams | Ease of use — no new tool, no new login, nothing to adopt separately. |
