# Lightning demo — 10 minutes

The short cut of the [full walkthrough](DEMO-WALKTHROUGH.md), which runs 30 minutes.
Same product, same claims, fewer stops. If you have not driven the product before, read
the walkthrough first — it names every screen and assumes no technical knowledge.

**Say this before you click anything:**

> "Everything you see is a demonstration book — invented companies, invented people,
> invented numbers, and the market and news panels are demonstration data too. The
> product connects to live public filings and news; nothing on screen today is coming
> from outside."

---

## The ten minutes

| # | Beat | What to do | What it proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Home** · 2 min | Read the **daily briefing** at the top — prose, not a chart. Press **🔍 Evidence**; every numbered claim opens to its source. Then point at **Untracked follow-ups**: *"39 promises made in Teams channels that nobody wrote down. It does not chase them. It tells you they exist."* | Everything shows its working, and it finds what no one is tracking. |
| **2** | **Sourcing & screening** · 1 min | Open the **Sourcing framework** sub-tab. **Fund mandate** is the hard box every target must fit — outside it, a company is excluded and never scored. **Investment themes** are a partner's hunting ground — they shape the search and exclude nothing. | It knows the difference between a rule from your investors and a partner's judgement. |
| **3** | **Inside a deal** · 3 min | **Deals in flight** → **Helvetia Diagnostics**. Give it fifteen seconds. Read the **Where to start** line above the tabs. Open **Thesis & key figures**: the IC readiness card says two obligations outstanding and lists exactly two; hover a figure for its source. Open **Diligence workstreams**: the header says **"4 closed at IC, no write-up"**. | The numbers agree with each other, and the product is honest about a records gap. |
| **4** | **The assistant** · 1 min | **💬 Ask the assistant**, still inside the deal. Ask *"what is outstanding before we can close?"* Note the **Focus** box, set to this deal. Then say the line: *"The assistant drafts. The IC readiness tab is the record. Nothing reaches a committee paper unchecked."* | Grounded in this deal only, and honest about where authority sits. |
| **5** | **Different people** · 2 min | Top-left dropdown → **Chidi Anagonye — Analyst**. Deals in flight drops from 15 to 3. A deal he is not cleared for shows as **"Status only · Beacon Hill Biotech 🔒 · You are not on this deal team"**. The **Value & Exit 0** filter is greyed, not hidden. *"The restricted deal is never sent to the browser. There is nothing to inspect and nothing to switch back on."* | Access is real, and the product tells you when it is holding something back. |
| **6** | **Report** · 1 min | Open **Report**. It says **"Draft — not certified"** with a **✓ Certify for LP use** button, and the header states **"Market data: seeded"**. *"Nothing reaches an investor until a named person certifies it, and that freezes a dated copy that cannot be edited. And it prints on its own face which of its inputs are not live."* | It will not let you send an uncertified number, and it declares its own limits. |

**The three-minute cut:** beats **1 → 3 → 5**. The briefing that shows its working, the
numbers that agree, and access that is real. That is the whole story.

## Close

> "One place to run a deal from a first look to owning the company, inside the tools your
> team already uses. Every number tells you where it came from, and each person sees only
> what their role permits."

**Then ask for something.** A follow-up session with the deal team, or a conversation
about loading their own deals. A demo that ends without an ask ends without a decision.

## What it does for a private equity firm

| What you showed | The problem it addresses |
|---|---|
| The briefing, with **🔍 Evidence** on every claim | "Where did this number come from?" — the question that stops every committee. Each figure opens to the workstream, filing or paper behind it. |
| **Untracked follow-ups** | Commitments made in a channel and never written down. Every firm has this and no way to see it. |
| **Fund mandate** as a hard box, **investment themes** as a guide | Time wasted on targets the fund was never permitted to buy. |
| **IC readiness** — required papers, blocking workstreams, conditions | IC preparation that is defensible rather than a progress bar. |
| **The assistant, scoped and subordinate** | "Can I trust the AI?" — it drafts from this deal's record, cites its sources, and never overrides the tab that holds the record. |
| **Role-based access, enforced before the data is sent** | Information barriers, deal-team confidentiality, LP and regulatory separation. Not a display setting. |
| **Report — draft until certified** | Nothing reaches an investor without a named person freezing a dated copy. |
| **Fund & Portfolio** | The same platform owns the company after the deal closes, not just up to it. |
| **Runs in Teams and in a browser, on your own tenant** | No new tool to learn; the data room is your SharePoint and the conversations are your Teams channels. |
| **Settings — every outside source switchable** | You decide what the product is allowed to look at. |
